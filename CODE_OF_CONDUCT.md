# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to everyone who participates in the Qryx community: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## In short

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people, including newcomers and non-native English speakers

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments and personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer, [@0605AbMu](https://github.com/0605AbMu), using the contact details on their GitHub profile. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers will follow the [Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) when deciding on consequences.
